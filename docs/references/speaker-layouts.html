Ambisonics Formats

Ambisonics formats use Spherical Harmonic (SH) encoding, not discrete speaker positions. Channel count follows (order+1)². ACN channel ordering, SN3D normalization. Decoding to physical speakers is handled downstream by the renderer.

FormatOrderChannelsFormula
FOA (1st Order)14(1+1)² = 4
SOA (2nd Order)29(2+1)² = 9
HOA (3rd Order)316(3+1)² = 16
4OA (4th Order)425(4+1)² = 25
5OA (5th Order)536(5+1)² = 36
6OA (6th Order)649(6+1)² = 49
