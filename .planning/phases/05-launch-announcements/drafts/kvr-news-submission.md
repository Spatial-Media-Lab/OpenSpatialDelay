## Press-release body (250–500 words)

Spatial Media Lab today released OpenSpatialDelay v1.0.0, a free GPL-3.0 spatial delay plugin for VST3 and AU on macOS and Windows. Each echo has a 3D position in space. OpenSpatialDelay is the first tool in the Spatial Media Library pipeline — an open-source collection of spatial audio tools for musicians, sound designers, and immersive-audio practitioners.

The plugin works in stereo today and grows with your mix into binaural, surround, or Atmos whenever the session calls for it. It ships with 12 independently-positioned delay taps, three algorithm families, and 70 factory presets — including **Stereo Ping-Pong** (two taps at ±90° for the classic psychoacoustic effect) and **Wide Stereo** (two taps at a ±30° tempo-synced stereo pair). The three algorithm families share the same 12-tap UI and the same preset bank:

- **Stereo (5 modes):** Equal Power, Stereo VBAP, XY Pair, MS Encode, Blumlein — load it on any stereo bus and it works immediately
- **Binaural (6 modes):** five measured HRTF profiles (KU100, CIPIC, HUTUBS, MIT KEMAR, SADIE) plus a CPU-lite Simple mode — headphone spatial delay on any stereo track, no surround output bus required
- **Surround / Immersive (7 algorithms):** Ambisonics, ConstantPower, DBAP, KNN, MDAP, VBAP, VBIP — covering Quad, 5.1, 7.1.4 Atmos, domes, and Ambisonics up to 6th order

The plugin also includes a trajectory engine (13 path shapes), ADM-OSC send and receive for integration with Spat Revolution, Panoramix, Iannix, and TouchDesigner, and a full factory preset bank of 70 patches spanning stereo production, headphone pieces, cinematic design, and Atmos sessions.

OpenSpatialDelay is developed by Andrew Rahman, a Berlin-based spatial media expert and co-founder of Spatial Media Lab (with Timo Bittner). It's the first piece of software Andrew has shipped — built over seven weeks starting March 6, 2026. The plugin is funded through Andrew's Patreon, which supports the full Spatial Media Library pipeline. The source is open under GPL-3.0.
